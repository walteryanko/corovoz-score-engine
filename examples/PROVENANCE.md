# Fixture provenance

`synthetic-satb.musicxml` was written for this public engineering excerpt. It is an original one-measure 4/4 sustained chord with four named SATB parts, no lyrics and no copied score source. The test suite constructs its additional timing, repeat, malformed-XML and compressed-MXL inputs from this synthetic material or small original strings.

These first-party fixtures are included in the repository's MPL-2.0 scope. No real user's upload, copyrighted arrangement, private recording or external notation-book page is included.
